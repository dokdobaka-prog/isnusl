What we found

Results

We compared the same type of model before and after adding 120 examples with negation.

200 real test reviews
84.5% → 81.0%

Overall accuracy decreased after adding examples.

45 real reviews with negation keywords
75.6% → 75.6%

Accuracy stayed the same in this small group.

60 synthetic test sentences
41.7% → 93.3%

The artificial test reused key word pairs from training.

Our conclusion. The new examples helped on similar artificial sentences, but did not improve overall accuracy on real reviews. A bigger, more varied test is needed.
